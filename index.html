About

Balasubramanian's path runs through both eras of the web stack. He cut his teeth on PHP, Laravel and CodeIgniter — production systems at EURYS and INSAKAL — then carried that grounding into React, Node.js and MongoDB for full-stack builds at TTK and, most recently, SEQUIFI.

He started as an intern at QR Solutions in Trichy in 2017, and has since worked across Chennai and Bangalore. Whichever half of the stack a project leans on, his toolset — Tailwind, Bootstrap, Redux Toolkit, REST APIs, MySQL and MongoDB — is sized to meet it.
